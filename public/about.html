About

This site brings together news articles from different sources and presents them in a simple, reader-focused format.

Articles are ranked based on reader votes. The aim is to highlight the stories that readers consider important, rather than relying solely on popularity or engagement.

Articles remain the property of their respective publishers. This site provides links to the original articles and does not claim ownership of their content.

Disclaimer: The news articles are not realtime. All articles are fetched at 07:00 AM IST everyday. Only the articles fetched on the same date are shown. The content on the site are more similar to a newpaper which has presents a fixed set of artilces, rather than real time news websites or applications.

Privacy Policy

This site collects only information reasonably necessary to operate and protect its features.

Information collected

When voting, the site may process a randomly generated visitor identifier stored in the browser. This identifier is used to prevent the same browser from repeatedly voting for the same article.

The site may also process the IP address associated with a voting request. This is used to enforce limits on voting activity and help prevent abuse of the voting system.

Cookies and local storage

The site may use browser local storage to remember voting activity and related preferences. This information remains in the browser unless it is cleared.

Links on this site may lead to third-party websites. Those websites have their own privacy policies and terms. This site is not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites.

Data retention

Information associated with voting may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the voting system, prevent abuse, maintain rankings, and protect the service.
